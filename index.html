About the brand

What makes NuTriLeaf different?

NuTriLeaf comes from Nutritious + Trio + Leaf — inspired by the three leafy vegetables at the heart of the product.

Saluyot

A locally familiar leafy ingredient that helps give NuTriLeaf its distinctive vegetable identity.

Malunggay

A well-known local leafy vegetable that forms part of the product's three-leaf concept.

Spinach

Combined with saluyot and malunggay to create the base vegetable blend of the crackers.

Our concept

From local leaves to a new kind of crunch.

From ingredients to pack

How NuTriLeaf is made.

A simplified overview of the production process described in the feasibility study.

STEP 01

Prepare

Ingredients and equipment are prepared and cleaned before production.

STEP 02

Blend the leaves

Saluyot, malunggay, spinach, garlic powder, salt, and water are combined into a vegetable mixture.

STEP 03

Mix with rice flour

Rice flour is added and mixed with the vegetable mixture to form the product base.

STEP 04

Form & steam

The mixture is formed and steamed before the next preparation stages.

STEP 05

Dry

The formed product is dried to help achieve the desired crispiness and reduce excess moisture.

STEP 06

Fry

The dried pieces are fried to create the final crispy texture.

STEP 07

Season

The finished crackers are seasoned according to the selected variant.

STEP 08

Pack

The finished product is placed in clean, sealed packaging for protection from moisture and contamination.
